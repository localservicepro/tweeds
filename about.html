Our story

Property Maintenance That Understands the Tweed Coast

Based at 8 William Street, Murwillumbah NSW, we're right in the heart of the Tweed Shire — and we know first-hand what it takes to keep a property looking its best here. The Tweed Coast is beautiful, but it's tough on homes.

Fast-growing subtropical lawns, salt-laden sea air and humid summers mean mould on driveways, grimy coastal windows and gardens that get away from you quickly. So we built our services around exactly those local conditions — regular mowing rounds, pressure washing that targets coastal salt and mould, streak-free window cleaning, and a handyman to keep everything else in order.

The result is one trusted local contact for homeowners, retirees, landlords and property managers right across Murwillumbah, Tweed Heads, Banora Point, Pottsville, Kingscliff, Terranora and Cabarita.
