What does a handyman do?

A handyman handles the repairs and small jobs around a property that don't need a specialist trade — fixings, minor carpentry, gutter clearing, minor painting and general maintenance. Tweed Property Maintenance covers the whole list across Murwillumbah and Tweed Heads. Call 0448 287 547.
