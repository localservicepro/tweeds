Overview

Streak-Free Glass for Coastal Homes & Holiday Rentals

Sea spray and salt haze film up coastal glass fast — and nothing dulls a Tweed Coast view like grimy windows. We clean glass inside and out until it's crystal clear and streak-free, then wipe down tracks, sills and flyscreens so the whole window looks new.

We're a favourite with holiday-rental owners and property managers around Kingscliff and Cabarita for fast changeover window cleans between guest stays. Bundle window cleaning with pressure washing in the same visit and save yourself a second call-out.

Yes — Tweed Property Maintenance cleans windows inside and out, including glass, frames, tracks and flyscreens, for homes across Kingscliff, Cabarita, Pottsville and the Tweed Coast. Streak-free results, free quotes — call 0448 287 547.
