Overview

Coastal-Grade Exterior Cleaning for Tweed Coast Properties

Life near the water is hard on surfaces. Salt air, humidity and shade leave driveways streaked with black mould, render dulled with grime and pathways slippery. Our pressure washing strips it all back — restoring the original colour of concrete, pavers, brick and cladding across Tweed Heads and Banora Point.

We match the right pressure and technique to each surface — high pressure for hard concrete and driveways, gentler soft-wash methods for render, painted walls and roofs — so everything comes up clean without damage. It's the fastest way to lift street appeal before a sale, inspection or open home.

Can you remove black mould and salt from coastal homes?
Absolutely — coastal salt, black mould and algae are exactly what we deal with daily on Tweed Coast properties. We use the right pressure and treatments to remove buildup and slow its return.
Is pressure washing safe for render and painted walls?
Yes. For delicate surfaces like render, painted walls and roofs we use lower-pressure soft-wash techniques that clean thoroughly without stripping paint or damaging the surface.
