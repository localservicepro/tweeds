How often should I mow my lawn in Northern NSW?
Through the warm, wet Northern NSW growing season most lawns do best mowed weekly to fortnightly. In cooler months a monthly cut is often enough. We'll recommend a schedule that keeps your lawn healthy without over-cutting.
